# Code of conduct

Applies to every repository in the Morgenruf organisation, and to the issues, pull
requests and discussions in them.

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume they are acting in good faith. Argue with the idea rather
than the person holding it.

## What is expected

- Treat disagreement as normal. Most technical questions have more than one defensible
  answer, and the person who disagrees with you has usually thought about it.
- Give feedback on the work. "This will break when the token expires" is useful.
  "This is amateur" is not.
- Accept feedback the same way. A review that finds a problem has saved you a bug.
- Remember that most people here are contributing in time they were not paid for, in
  a language that may not be their first.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, including anything targeting someone's identity.
- Sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or repeatedly reopening a settled decision to
  wear people down.

## Scope

This applies in the repositories, in issues, pull requests and discussions, and
anywhere someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a problem to **conduct@morgenruf.dev**. Reports go to the maintainers and stay
confidential.

Tell us what happened, where, and when. Links help. You do not need to have a view on
what the response should be.

You will get an acknowledgement within three working days. You will not be asked to
confront the other person, and reporting something in good faith will never count
against you.

## What happens next

Maintainers will look into it and decide on a response. Depending on what happened
that may be a private word, a public correction, removing a comment, or a temporary
or permanent ban from the project.

Maintainers are held to this document too, and more strictly rather than less. A
report about a maintainer is handled by the others.
